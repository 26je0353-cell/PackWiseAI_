Method

How the scoring engine works

A transparent, rule-weighted model rather than a black box — every score traces back to a stated reason, which matters for a food-safety-adjacent recommendation.

1

Profile the commodity

Moisture, oxygen and light sensitivity, storage temperature and target shelf life are converted into weighted requirement scores.

2

Score the material library

Each of the 13 packaging profiles is rated on barrier performance, temperature range, cost and sustainability; requirements are matched against these ratings.

3

Rank and explain

Materials are ranked by weighted fit, and the top matches are returned with the specific factors that drove the score.
